# SciCode collection notes

- **What it measures:** SciCode evaluates language models on scientific research coding. The source describes 80 main problems decomposed into 338 subproblems, curated across scientific fields. Main-problem and subproblem resolve rates are reported both without and with supplementary background information.
- **Captured version and date:** Kaggle Open Benchmarks SciCode suite, version `v1`; leaderboard last updated **2026-09-14**. Retrieved **2026-09-23 UTC** (2026-09-22 in the collector's EDT timezone).
- **Collection method:** Captured the JavaScript-rendered suite page with Firecrawl. The raw capture `kaggle-rendered.md` contains the suite table; the four individual Kaggle category page captures are retained as cross-checks. Also saved the official SciCode leaderboard HTML, repository metadata/tree, and repository license. Kaggle's page is the freshest source located; the official SciCode website page currently has an older table and older update notes.
- **Metrics:** `main_problem_resolve_rate` is the published SciCode Main score; `subproblem_resolve_rate` is the published SciCode Subproblem score. `subset` records standard evaluation versus evaluation with background. Values and `ci` are copied from the four suite columns as percentage points; `ci` is the displayed ± half-width. Kaggle does not state the interval's confidence level or a per-row task count in the captured table. The table publishes no paired cost, time, effort, or harness information.
- **Coverage:** 55 visible model configurations × 4 score columns = **220 rows**. Kaggle says 57 models are evaluated but the suite page renders 55 of 57; its individual metric pages render 52 of 54. The two not-rendered configurations have no recoverable names or scores in the public captures, so they are not guessed or included. Some suite-page ranking entries carry score 0.0; those are retained as published values.
- **Caveats:** Results are reported through Kaggle Open Benchmarks and were not independently reproduced by this collection. The captured leaderboard does not identify each score as vendor-reported or independently run, so provenance by model is unknown. The SciCode authors' official page shows a much older and smaller leaderboard (20 entries in the captured page); per-model run dates are absent, so older entries may be stale despite the suite update date. The benchmark tasks, test cases, and gold solutions have public releases, creating potential contamination exposure; this capture cannot determine whether any model training or evaluation was contaminated. SciCode-Verified (arXiv:2608.04975) reports benchmark defects; interpret scores against the captured version and that paper's caveats. Kaggle's displayed intervals may not describe all sources of uncertainty.
- **License:** The captured SciCode repository license is Apache-2.0 (`raw/scicode/repository-LICENSE.txt`). No separate redistribution license for Kaggle leaderboard results was stated on the captured page; row `license` is therefore null.
- **Not captured:** The names and scores of the two models omitted by Kaggle's 55-of-57 display, interval definition/confidence level, task counts per result, and any run dates before the September 14 leaderboard update.
