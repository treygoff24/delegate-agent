# Routing findings, 2026-09-22

This file reads the first model-bench sweep (68 benchmarks, 72,570 rows) against the fleet we route to. The generated tables are in `2026-09-22.md`. Numbers here come from those rows. Where the text draws a conclusion from them, it says so.

## Sources that carry the weight

Most benchmarks list each model once, at one effort. Three sources measure the fleet at several efforts and attach cost, and they drive most of what follows:

- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.** Every fleet model at every effort it exposes, with cost per task and median response time. It has subscores for terminal coding, office work (GDPval), long context, science, hallucination, and multimodal.
- **Vals Index.** A knowledge-work index with cost and latency on every row, plus parts that include Terminal-Bench 2.1, a finance agent, and Vibe Code Bench.
- **ARC-AGI.** Accuracy and cost per task at each effort.

GPT-6 Sol and Luna are about a week old. Only about five benchmarks list them, mostly Artificial Analysis. Opus 5.5 has seven. Their independent cross-checks are thin, and the conclusions about them rest mainly on Artificial Analysis.

## Findings

**1. GPT-6 Luna is far weaker than the fleet notes assume.** On the Artificial Analysis index, Luna at max scores 37.3, Sol at medium 39.8, and Opus 5.5 at medium 51.2. Luna's Terminal-Bench 4 score is 8% at xhigh and 13% at max, against 30% for Sol at xhigh and 60% for Opus 5.5 at xhigh. Its long-context score is fine (83% at max, level with Sol) and its cost is tiny (about $0.04 per task at xhigh). Conclusion: Luna fits wide research, reading, summarizing, and extraction. It should not write code unless something stronger reviews it. The fleet note that Luna xhigh or max is close to Opus medium is contradicted by this data.

**2. Opus 5.5 is the strongest general model measured, and good value at high effort.** On the Artificial Analysis index Opus 5.5 at max scores 57.6, above Fable 5.1 at max (53.4) and GPT-6 Astra at max (52.7). It also has the top Vals Index score (69.7). Opus 5.5 at high scores 53.6 at $1.8 per task. Sol's best is 47.5 at max, at $1.1. On Vals' Terminal-Bench 2.1, Opus 5.5 at high leads at 87.6% for $0.45 per task, ahead of Astra at max (87.3%, $1.34) and Fable at high (85.0%, $3.07). Frontier-SWE agrees (Opus 5.5 0.90, Fable 0.84, Astra 0.74).

**3. Astra and Fable remain the apex, on different axes.** Astra leads math (MathArena 88, ARC-AGI 95%), the Code Arena votes, creative writing by a hair, the Remote Labor Index (20.8% automation versus Fable's 17.9%), and Vending-Bench. Fable leads the fleet head-to-head across most knowledge categories: legal, finance, human preference, MCP tool use (87%), and SimpleBench (87%).

**4. MiMo v2.6 Pro is the value standout.** It scores 46.3 on the Artificial Analysis index at $0.13 per task (Grok 4.7 is 46.3 at $2.7) and 59.7 on the Vals Index at $0.39 for the full suite. It is slow: a 46 s median response, and the slowest completion times in Vals.

**5. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is a strong cheap coder that hallucinates.** It scores 74.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at $0.10 per task, 84.7% on Vibe Code Bench at $0.41, and 57.9 on the Vals Index at $0.30. Its Artificial Analysis non-hallucination score is 0.035, the lowest in the fleet (Grok 4.7 is 0.71). Conclusion: use it where tests or a verifier catch invented facts.

**6. Muse Spark 1.3 is better than its trial status suggests.** It scores 64.5 on the Vals Index at max for $3.4, behind only the three apex models, which cost $19–29. It tops PRBench legal (61.6) and is near the top of the Vals finance agent. Its Terminal-Bench scores are middling (33% on Terminal-Bench 4 at max, 79% on Terminal-Bench 2.1).

**7. Gemini 3.8 Flash is a knowledge-work model, not a coder.** It tops the Vals finance agent (61.4%), comes second on LegalBench (87%) and the Finance Benchmark index, and scores 82% on SimpleBench. On agentic coding it is last or near last among fleet models (Terminal-Bench 4 19%, Artificial Analysis terminal subscore 20%). It also answers the most sensitive prompts in the SpeechMap refusal test (86% complete).

**8. Grok 4.7 is the most factual model but a middling coder.** It has the fleet's best non-hallucination score (0.71 at xhigh) and strong office-work scores (GDPval 0.60). Its Terminal-Bench 4 score is 26–38% depending on source, and it sits mid-pack in the fleet head-to-head.

**9. Kimi K3 leads long context.** It leads AA-LCR at 88.7% and leads the fleet on the one multimodal benchmark with three or more fleet models.

**10. MiniMax M3 trails everywhere.** It ranks last or near last in nearly every head-to-head category.

## What this suggests for routing

These are proposals for Trey to rule on, not changes made:

- Demote Luna from the "close to Opus medium" workhorse tier to a cheap research-and-reading tier. Keep using it heavily there: that is where its price matters.
- Make Opus 5.5 at high the default frontier workhorse for coding and agentic work. It beats Sol at every effort for similar per-task cost and is the most cost-efficient frontier Terminal-Bench result measured.
- Sol keeps a place as the family-decorrelated second opinion and for Codex-native tasks, not as the default.
- Promote MiMo from overnight curiosity to the cheap-quality bulk lane whenever latency does not matter.
- Keep DeepSeek V4.1 Flash for tested code. Keep it away from unverified factual writing.
- Consider graduating Muse from its coding-only trial: the data says it is broader than coding.
- Route Gemini 3.8 Flash to finance, legal, and document work. Take it off coding lanes.

## Known gaps and caveats

- Sol, Luna, and Opus 5.5 have few independent benchmarks yet. The next refresh should see many more.
- Public benchmarks run through each vendor's API or a benchmark's own harness. The Codex and Claude Code harnesses we use may shift results, especially for agentic coding.
- The fleet head-to-head ranks each model at its best listed effort. It compares ceilings, not price-matched configurations. The effort ladders and the notes above cover price.
- WebDev Arena and Code Arena are one leaderboard. WebDev Arena is marked `superseded_by` and excluded.
- Vectara's hallucination rows show 100 for GPT-6 Astra. That is implausible as a hallucination rate and needs checking before anyone cites it.
- FinQA's verification failed: its source dates predate the models named. Its 8 rows carry little weight.
